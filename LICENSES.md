# Rights and upstream assets

This release makes the authors' research artifact publicly accessible. The
authors' code, scripts, documentation, and derived aggregate results in this
repository are released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). The manuscript
sources are included for reproducibility; the paper text itself is distributed
under the terms of the venues where it appears. Existing notices in supplied
files remain applicable.

Upstream benchmark rows, model weights, and raw provider responses are not
redistributed or relicensed here. Their owners' licenses and terms govern access
and use. Source URLs, pinned revisions, and checksums are documented in
`artifact/README.md`, the protocol files, and the source manifests.

- **LinuxArena public data:** the manuscript records CC BY 4.0 for the upstream
  public sample. The pinned dataset is
  `anonymouslinuxarena/linuxarena-public`, revision
  `2fd29f9c7bc190fb873bd76c6bd648f22b9ab0a7`.
- **ToolSafe / TS-Bench and AgentHarm-derived inputs:** the audited repositories
  did not expose complete standard-license information for all assets. This
  release does not assume permission to redistribute those inputs. Fetching an
  input is subject to its upstream rights and terms.
- **TS-Guard and other checkpoints:** obtain separately from the documented
  upstream source under the applicable model terms.
- **NeurIPS style:** `neurips_2026.sty` retains its original header and notices.
- **Python dependencies:** install from their upstream distributions; their own
  licenses apply.

The paper's checklist therefore continues to state that complete licensing
documentation for all existing assets is unavailable.
